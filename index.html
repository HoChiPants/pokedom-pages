Welcome to the grove.

Pokedom is an original creature strategy game for iPhone. Build an eight-card deck, defend your towers and grow your collection through offline battles.

Need a hand?

For questions, bugs or privacy requests, contact Studio Minden.

pokedom@studiominden.org

A useful bug report includes what happened, the steps to repeat it, your app version/build, iOS version and device model. Screenshots are optional.

What is in this beta?

Play against computer opponents with 33 authored cards: 18 base creatures, 12 mature forms, two spells and a defensive Wardpost. Edit your eight-card deck, take on 36 campaign missions across six chapters with six chapter bosses, and earn Seeds and Renown for your collection.

Choose from six cosmetic skins using earned Seeds. Buying a skin saves its ownership locally; equipping it or returning to the original appearance is free and keeps the skin in your collection. Skins do not change combat stats or abilities.

This build has no accounts, real-money purchases, advertising or online battles. Player-created sprites, names and move sets, imports, AI tools and player sharing are not available in this version.

Your camp saves locally

Your deck, collection upgrades, campaign progress, rewards, owned skins, equipped appearances and preferences save on this device. There is no in-app cloud sync or cross-device account. Deleting the app and its data can remove your camp. An active battle does not resume after the app is terminated; leaving a battle early earns no reward.

Testing through TestFlight

If you have received an invitation, use Apple’s TestFlight app to access the beta. Access depends on your invitation and the build’s availability; this page is not a download link.

Beta features and balance can change. Apple’s TestFlight service also collects diagnostic and testing information separately from the offline game. Read the privacy policy before testing.

Contact from inside the app

Open Settings → Contact Support to prepare an email. Its clearly marked Diagnostics section contains only the app name, version/build, iOS version and generic device model. You can review or edit it before sending. The app does not send the message automatically or silently attach logs or game data.

These static support pages use no cookies, analytics, advertising or external assets. See the privacy policy for the hosting provider’s separate security logs.
